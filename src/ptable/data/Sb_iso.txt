Main article: Isotopes of antimony
Antimony has two stable isotopes: 121Sb with a natural abundance of 57.21% and 123Sb with a natural abundance of 42.79%. There are 37 artificial radioactive isotopes known with mass numbers 104 to 142, of which the longest-lived is the fission product 125Sb with a half-life of 2.758&#160;years. Numerous meta states are known, of which the most stable is 120m1Sb with a half-life of 5.76&#160;days. Isotopes that are lighter than the stable 123Sb tend to undergo β+ decay, and those that are heavier experience β− decay, with some exceptions.
