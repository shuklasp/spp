<p><a href="/school1/ptable/element/Bi" class="wiki-link" title="Bismuth (Bi)">Bismuth</a> has few commercial applications, and those that use it generally require small quantities relative to other raw materials. In the United States, for example, 733 tonnes of bismuth were consumed in 2016, of which 70% went into chemicals (including pharmaceuticals, pigments, and cosmetics) and 11% into bismuth alloys.
</p>
